ABOUT SOPRIMICO

Built for people who want to understand.

SoPrimico is a cybersecurity platform focused on learning, security research, useful tools and responsible information gathering.

What is SoPrimico?

SoPrimico is a project created around cybersecurity, ethical hacking and technical research.

Our goal is to make cybersecurity knowledge more accessible by bringing together educational resources, security tools, tutorials and research services in one place.

Whether you are starting from zero or already have experience with cybersecurity, SoPrimico is designed to help you learn, experiment and improve your technical knowledge.
