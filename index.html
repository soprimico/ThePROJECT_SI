What is SoPrimico?

SoPrimico is a cybersecurity-focused platform dedicated to providing tools, educational resources and research services for ethical hacking and information security.

Our goal is to make cybersecurity knowledge more accessible while promoting responsible and authorized security testing.

SoPrimico Research Center

Our Research Center provides cybersecurity research and investigation services for clients who require professional assistance.

Depending on the project, we can investigate security incidents, analyze publicly available information, perform authorized security assessments and conduct cybersecurity research.

All investigations must have a legitimate purpose and the appropriate authorization. We do not conduct unauthorized surveillance, harassment or illegal access to systems or accounts.
